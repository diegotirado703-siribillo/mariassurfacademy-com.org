How a lesson works

Every lesson is at Marias Beach in Rincón and lasts about 2 hours, depending on conditions.

Get your board

Your board is included in the price.

Land lesson

About 20 minutes on the beach for a land lesson and wave orientation.

Catch waves

1 to 1.5 hours in the water catching waves with your instructors.

The number of instructors depends on the conditions and how many people are in the lesson.

Local surf coaches, born to this water

Marias Surf Academy is a local school in Rincón. Every lesson is at Marias Beach, and we know its waves and conditions well.

Our water men and women are with you from the first paddle to the last wave.

  • CPR and lifeguard certifiedEvery coach is trained to respond in the water and on the beach.
  • Lessons for every ageChildren, beginners, advanced surfers and older adults. We adapt the pace and the waves to you.
  • Attention on youSmall groups and private coaching, so your coach is always watching you.
